# Tally

Training, food and body, counted one mark at a time.

Tally is a gym and diet tracker. It runs as an Android app (Capacitor) and as a web app, and everything stays on the phone.

- Workout plans I can change on the spot: swap, reorder, add or remove exercises and sets, for one session or for good
- Food and protein tracking without a food scale
- Progress over time, with a weekly trend instead of daily noise
- Reminders that fire on the lock screen
- A Ramadan mode for fasting months

**Built with:** JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Capacitor (Android), service worker for offline use.
